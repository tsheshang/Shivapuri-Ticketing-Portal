About the Park

Nepal's closest wilderness escape from the capital

A Forest at the Capital's Doorstep

Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park sits just north of Kathmandu, spanning dense hill forests, sacred springs, and the source of the Bagmati River. Established to protect the watershed supplying the valley, it's home to leopards, Himalayan black bears, and hundreds of bird species.

🥾

Hiking Trails

Well-marked trails ranging from short nature walks to the demanding Shivapuri Peak hike.

🦅

Wildlife & Birding

Over 300 recorded bird species, plus leopards, deer, and Himalayan black bear.

💧

Bagmati Source

The sacred origin point of the Bagmati River lies within the park's protected zone.
